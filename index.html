This Privacy Policy explains how nullpixelstudios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Chaos Cadre, including its sign-in and account system (guest, Google Play Games, and email accounts), online multiplayer, friends, lobby chat, and account deletion. The Android package name is com.nullpixel.chaoscadre.

1. Information we process

  • Accounts and sign-in: Your internal account identifier, public player ID, nickname, account creation information, sign-in method (guest, Google Play Games, or email), and authentication/session information such as session and refresh tokens. A guest account uses a randomly generated identifier stored by the game on your device. See Section 2 for details.
  • Google Play Games: When you sign in or link an account through Google Play Games, we process the Google-linked player identity and authorization information needed to authenticate you and associate your game profile with that account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Email sign-in: If you register or sign in with an email address, we process the email address and password you provide. The game sends them to our authentication service over HTTPS to create, link, or sign in to the account; the game does not save your password on your device as a saved login. We store your email address with your account to identify it for sign-in and support.
  • Gameplay and progress: Match participation and results, scores, kills, deaths, wins, rankings, progression, selected game modes, matchmaking information, and lobby membership.
  • Social features: Friend requests, accepted friendships, blocking relationships, invitations, online/activity status, and the messages you send in lobby chat, including sender identifiers and timestamps.
  • Connection and service information: Your IP address is processed when you connect to our services. The server also uses hashed-IP records to limit excessive requests and prevent abuse. We also process connection timing, server/region selections, latency measurements, and the technical information needed to operate multiplayer and prevent misuse. Additional operational logging is described under retention below.
  • Advertising and analytics (not active in the current release): If and when these services are enabled in a future version, device or app identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and app usage information as explained in Section 6.
  • Device storage and support: The game stores settings, local progress, guest identifiers, and saved login/session information on your device. If you contact us (for example for account recovery or deletion), we receive the contact details and information you choose to include in your request, and we may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify account ownership.

2. Accounts, sign-in, and guest play

Chaos Cadre uses an account system so that your profile, progress, rank, and social features are tied to an identity you control. You can use the game in the following ways:

  • Guest account. On first launch the game can create a guest account automatically. A guest account is identified by a randomly generated identifier stored on your device and a public player ID. Guest accounts may have limited access: some online features, such as friends, parties, lobby chat, and ranked play, require a signed-in (Google Play Games or email) account. Offline modes do not require an account.
  • Google Play Games sign-in. You can sign in with, or link, your Google Play Games player identity. Google handles the sign-in. We receive the player identity and authorization information needed to authenticate you and associate your game profile with it. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Email and password. You can register or sign in with an email address and password. We use your email address to identify your account for sign-in, to verify ownership when you contact us for support or deletion, and for messages directly related to your account or security. We do not use your email address for advertising or marketing and do not display it in public profiles.

Linking and upgrading a guest account

If you link a guest account to Google Play Games or an email address, your existing profile, player ID, progress, and ranked data stay with the account and are associated with that sign-in method. Linking is how you keep your account if you reinstall the game or change devices.

Guest account risk: because a guest account is identified only by a random identifier stored on your device, clearing the game's data, uninstalling it, or switching devices can permanently remove your access to that guest account and its progress unless you linked it first. We may not be able to recover an unlinked guest account.

Passwords, sessions, and device storage

  • Your password is sent to our authentication service over HTTPS and is stored by that service in protected (hashed) form rather than as plain text. We do not display or have a way to read your password. Choose a strong, unique password and do not share it. Never send your password to us or anyone else by email or chat.
  • After you sign in, the game stores a session (authentication and refresh) token on your device so you stay signed in. On the Android build with our native sign-in plugin, this saved login information is encrypted using a key protected by Android Keystore. Sessions can expire, in which case you must sign in again.
  • Signing out removes the saved session from your device. It does not delete your account; to delete the account, see Section 9.

Account security and recovery

You are responsible for activity under your account and for keeping your sign-in details secure. Contact us promptly if you think someone else is using your account. If you lose access, contact us at the address in Section 11. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that the account belongs to you, and if we cannot verify ownership we may be unable to restore or modify the account.

Account restrictions

We may limit, suspend, or remove accounts that cheat, abuse other players, abuse our services, or break the game's rules. To enforce these actions and prevent repeat abuse, we may keep the account identifier and related records needed for that purpose even after the account is deleted.

Age

You must be at least 13 years old to create or use an account. See Section 10.

We use this information to create and maintain game accounts (including guest, Google Play Games, and email accounts); authenticate players and keep them signed in; link guest accounts and restore linked profiles; verify account ownership for support and deletion requests; secure accounts and enforce account restrictions; save progress; run matches and rankings; enable profile searches, friends, invitations, and chat; maintain service reliability; prevent cheating or abuse; respond to support and privacy requests; and, only if those services are enabled in a future version, deliver and measure advertising and understand how players use the game to improve it.

4. Information visible to other players

Your public player ID, nickname, username, and game profile/rank information may be visible through searches, profiles, leaderboards, lobbies, and matches. Accepted friends can see your online/activity status. Lobby chat is visible to members of that lobby, including members who join while its recent messages remain available.

Your email address, password, sign-in method, and authentication tokens are not displayed in public game profiles. Avoid including private information in your nickname or chat messages. Other players may retain screenshots or copies of content they can see.

7. Storage, security, and international processing

Account information and online progress are stored on our server infrastructure. Sign-in, account, profile, and lobby-service requests use HTTPS, and passwords are stored by our authentication service in hashed form. On the Android build with our native sign-in plugin, saved login information is encrypted using a key protected by Android Keystore. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information outside your country. The protections available in those locations may differ from those in your country.

8. Retention

We retain account and progression data to maintain your account and provide the game, subject to account deletion and any necessary retention described below. The current lobby-chat system keeps a limited recent history of up to 50 messages per lobby in its live database; this is not a promise that every copy, backup, or recipient screenshot is immediately erased.

Account and sign-in records. We keep your account, email address (if provided), linked sign-in identities, and session records for as long as the account exists. We have not set a fixed deletion period for inactive guest or registered accounts; deleting your account (Section 9) removes it on request. Records kept to enforce account restrictions are described in Section 2.

Container logs. Running containers are configured to keep up to three log files of 10 MB each. Older logs rotate out as these size limits are reached. There is no fixed retention period in days, so the time a log entry remains depends on the volume of new logs. Logs may remain after an account is deleted for troubleshooting and service recovery.

Local backups. Covered local backups become eligible for deletion 30 days after their newest modification. The cleanup task runs hourly. This is a rule based on a backup file's modification time, not a guarantee that all information is erased exactly 30 days after account deletion. We have verified only the server-local backup configuration. Whether other copies exist, including downloaded backups, cloud-storage copies, or Oracle snapshots, and their deletion schedules have not been confirmed. Such copies are not covered by this local cleanup rule, and we cannot promise that they expire on the same schedule.

Deleted accounts in backups. Deleting an account does not rewrite or remove its information from existing backups. That information may remain in a backup until that backup is deleted under its applicable retention process. Backups are retained for recovery.

Rate-limit records. Hashed-IP rate-limit records help prevent abuse. Entries older than 24 hours are cleaned during request handling, rather than by a guaranteed deletion at exactly 24 hours.

Other player-linked records. We have not fully verified whether all player-linked records are removed from every database or other store after account deletion. Reset progress or an empty friend list confirms the visible result, not complete database removal. Please contact us about the status and scope of a particular deletion request.

Advertising and analytics records. The local log and backup schedules above do not set the retention periods of Unity or Google. Unity determines retention using the purposes for which information is processed and relevant legal and operational needs. Firebase Analytics user/event retention depends on the associated Google Analytics property settings; those settings do not necessarily determine retention of aggregated reports. Our selected Analytics retention setting has not yet been confirmed. See Google Analytics retention information and Unity's policy for their respective controls and criteria.

9. Delete your account or request help

Delete inside the game

Leave your party and finish any match, then open Online Hub → Account. Select the confirmation labeled "Permanently delete this ID, friends and ranked progress", then choose Delete Account. You may need to sign in again if your session has expired. Guest, Google Play Games, and email accounts can all be deleted this way. If you cannot sign in, use the email route below.

The deletion process is designed to remove your authentication account, any email address and linked sign-in identities stored with it, your game profile, ranked progress, and associated social records. Complete removal of every player-linked database record has not yet been verified. Operational records, third-party service records, and backups are subject to the limits described in Section 8. This action is permanent. Deleting your Chaos Cadre account does not delete your Google Account or your separate Google Play Games profile. Uninstalling the game or signing out does not delete the server account. A guest account that is deleted, or that you can no longer reach, cannot be recovered.

Request deletion without the game

Email thetrollstrike@gmail.com with the subject "Chaos Cadre account deletion". Include your public player ID (if you know it) and nickname. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that the account belongs to you before deleting it. Never send your password, login code, or authentication token. You do not need to reinstall the game to contact us.

Email an account-deletion request

This opens your email app. Send the message to submit your request. If no email app opens, write to the address above.

Email-request timeframe: Our team commits to completing your verified account-deletion request within 7 days after verifying account ownership. This is a support-team commitment, not an automatic server timer. Backup and log retention described in Section 8 continues to apply; the 7-day commitment does not mean every backup, log, or third-party record is erased within 7 days. The verification limits in Section 8 remain relevant when assessing what has actually been removed.

Deleting a game account does not automatically submit a deletion request to every advertising or analytics provider. Contact us about related data processed on our behalf; where a provider controls information independently, its privacy policy also provides request routes.

You can also use this contact to ask about your information, request corrections or access, or raise a privacy concern. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have additional rights, such as restricting or objecting to processing, withdrawing consent where processing relies on it, requesting portability, opting out of applicable targeted advertising or sale/sharing, or making a complaint to a relevant authority.
