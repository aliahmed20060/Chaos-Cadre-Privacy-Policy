The Game identifies reward amounts, qualifying actions and relevant limits in its progression, Operations and reward interfaces. Those offer-specific rules apply to the relevant action and must be available before a purchase or other decision that depends on them.

In server-run Quick Play, valid kills earn DC. Collecting qualifying power-ups contributes to additional DC, and scoring kills with additional different supported guns can earn a further DC bonus. Current combat earnings do not use collectible DC objects or a flat DC match-completion bonus.

Eligible Quick Play completion awards Quick XP based on kills and completion. Activity and completion requirements apply. An aborted round, early departure or insufficient activity can prevent completion-based rewards. Combat DC already confirmed by the server is not dependent on receiving the completion bonus. Unconfirmed events require successful server recovery and validation; a displayed effect alone is not proof that a wallet credit committed.

Level milestones can grant DC, Diamonds and identified cosmetic items to an account. Monthly Operations can award DC, Quick XP, Diamonds and a completion badge. Objectives progress together, awards are automatic when validated, and each objective reward is granted once for its period. Placement objectives require the displayed minimum of active human competitors; bots can still occupy scoreboard positions. Ties and match eligibility affect qualification.

Operations use calendar months in UTC and server event time. A month ending resets the opportunity to progress that month’s incomplete objectives; it does not itself erase currency already awarded or an earned badge. Current Operations and their amounts are shown in the Game. Earlier reward-road entitlements are preserved through the supported migration rules; a new reward schedule does not grant every past milestone again.

Quick XP stops increasing at the maximum level. That cap does not itself prevent a qualifying DC or Diamond reward. Local Play and Survival do not award account currency or Quick Play progression. Offline Quick Play has the separate guest progression described above. Rewards are virtual benefits, not wages, cash prizes or revenue-sharing payments.
